Corporate Operations
From paper to performance.

A 3-year transformation of corporate contracts and sales support at Alfa Scan — built from scratch, documented, automated, and made scalable.

2023
Manual
Paper documents. Phone calls. Individual memory. No tracking, no templates, no visibility.
2024
Structured
WhatsApp group for documented requests. Task-tracking sheets. Master data. Electronic templates with digital stamps. Around 90 price lists prepared, ~80% of companies renewed in H1.
2025
Digital
Google Form for all requests with auto-tracking. Historical data prepared in Excel and coordinated with the Application Team. 67 companies renewed in January 2025 alone.
2026
Performance-Driven
KPIs introduced in March 2026. Dashboards with customer segmentation, account owners, and monthly targets. Data tells the story now.
